## Benchmarks

- **dhrystone**: Dhrystone 2.1 (Weicker 1988), 10,000 runs; DMIPS = Dhrystones/s / 1757
- **whetstone**: Whetstone (Curnow & Wichmann 1972; C by Painter 1998), soft float, LOOP=4
- **richards**: Richards (Martin Richards), the original Count = 10,000
- **sieve**: BYTE Sieve of Eratosthenes (Gilbreath 1981), 10 iterations
- **stanford-bubblesort**: Stanford Bubblesort (Hennessy), 3 iterations
- **stanford-intmm**: Stanford IntMM (Hennessy), 2 iterations
- **stanford-perm**: Stanford Perm (Hennessy), 3 iterations
- **stanford-puzzle**: Stanford Puzzle (Hennessy), 1 iterations
- **stanford-queens**: Stanford Queens (Hennessy), 10 iterations
- **stanford-quicksort**: Stanford Quicksort (Hennessy), 4 iterations
- **stanford-towers**: Stanford Towers (Hennessy), 4 iterations
- **stanford-treesort**: Stanford Treesort (Hennessy), 2 iterations
- **stanford-floatmm**: Stanford FloatMM (Hennessy), 2 iterations
- **stanford-realmm**: Stanford RealMM (Hennessy), 1 iterations
- **stanford-oscar**: Stanford Oscar (Hennessy), 1 iterations
- **coremark**: CoreMark 1.0 (EEMBC 2009), 10 iterations: an ISA measurement, not a reportable score
- **doomdraw**: Doom's R_DrawColumn and R_DrawSpan in C, on a frame-like mix of walls and floors
- **doomdraw-asm**: The same with the hand-written R_DrawColumn and R_DrawSpan (doom/port/r_draw_fcpu.s)
- **basic-dhrystone**: Dhrystone 2.1 translated to BASIC, 100 runs, in the fpga_cpu BASIC interpreter
- **basic-bm1**: Rugg/Feldman BASIC benchmark 1 (Kilobaud 1977)
- **basic-bm2**: Rugg/Feldman BASIC benchmark 2 (Kilobaud 1977)
- **basic-bm3**: Rugg/Feldman BASIC benchmark 3 (Kilobaud 1977)
- **basic-bm4**: Rugg/Feldman BASIC benchmark 4 (Kilobaud 1977)
- **basic-bm5**: Rugg/Feldman BASIC benchmark 5 (Kilobaud 1977)
- **basic-bm6**: Rugg/Feldman BASIC benchmark 6 (Kilobaud 1977)
- **basic-bm7**: Rugg/Feldman BASIC benchmark 7 (Kilobaud 1977)
- **basic-bm8**: Rugg/Feldman BASIC benchmark 8 (Kilobaud 1977)
- **doom**: Doom (doomgeneric), -timedemo demo1 for 100 gametics, 32-bit int, expansion RAM
- **doom-asm**: Doom as above, with the hand-written R_DrawColumn and R_DrawSpan
